Precision in Pedagogy

This multi-year project involved the comprehensive design and technical production of a primary mathematics curriculum spanning six grade levels. The objective was to balance rigorous academic content with an engaging, accessible visual language that facilitates student learning.

The production process required meticulous attention to typesetting, where mathematical formulas and complex tables were integrated into a modular grid system. By utilizing advanced Adobe InDesign GREP styles and Master Pages, we achieved a consistent typographic rhythm across over 2,400 unique pages of content.
