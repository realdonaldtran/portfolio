Project Case Study

Digital Literacies

Advanced Typography & UI Design for Academic Publishing

Project Brief

The primary challenge was to architect a digital ecosystem that could host dense academic discourse without sacrificing the aesthetic pleasure of a physical literary journal. We aimed to balance complex data visualizations and multi-layered citations with an interface that feels quiet and authoritative.

"Design is the silent ambassador of your brand." — This project lived that philosophy by turning information density into a visual rhythm.

Design Philosophy

Intentional Restraint

Leveraging the Newsreader font, we focused on the negative space between glyphs to ensure maximum legibility. The palette is strictly Scholarly Green (#1A2F2B) on paper-toned backgrounds to reduce eye strain during prolonged reading.

Serif Focus Negative Space Tonal Layers
grid_on

Grid Systems

A strictly enforced 12-column fixed grid ensures that every marginalia and footnote aligns with the primary body text, creating a cohesive visual architecture across all devices.

height

Vertical Rhythm

Baseline grids were established to maintain a harmonic vertical flow. Leading is calculated specifically for the Newsreader typeface to optimize for continuous long-form reading.

accessibility

WCAG Compliance

Beyond aesthetics, the project achieves AA+ rating for contrast. Typography sizing is responsive, ensuring accessibility for vision-impaired scholars without breaking the grid.
