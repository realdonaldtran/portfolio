The Journey

Since entering the publishing world in the early 2000s, I have witnessed the evolution of the printed word from manual paste-ups to the sophisticated digital workflows of today. My career began with a deep appreciation for typography—the subtle rhythm of line spacing and the character of a well-cut serif.

Over the last 20 years, I have navigated the transitions from traditional CMYK production to XML-first workflows, always maintaining a "content-first" philosophy. Whether managing high-volume educational series or boutique art journals, my goal remains constant: to provide a seamless vessel for the author's voice.

Today, I bridge the gap between technical production management and creative vision, ensuring that every project is delivered with surgical precision and aesthetic excellence.
